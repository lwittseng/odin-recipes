Pizza

Dish Descirption

Pizza is a world renound dish, chances are you are probably reading this with a slice fo pizza in your hand. Hailing from Italy, but loved everywhere, pizza is a prime of example of why simple satisfies. In its purest form, a mixture of cheese, bread and tomato, it is bound to knock your socks off!

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • Cheese
  • Tomato
  • Peperoni

How to cook it

  1. Roll your bread flat.
  2. Sprinkle flour over the base.
  3. Cover base in tomato sauce and peperoni.
  4. Cook in the oven for 45 minutes to an hour.
